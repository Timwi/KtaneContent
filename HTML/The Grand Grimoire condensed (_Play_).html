First Spell of Pair
  • Group the first six spells from the module’s book into three pairs (1+2, 3+4, 5+6).
  • Use them in the table to the right, in order, to obtain the three candidates - C1, C2, and C3.
  • If C2 is the same as C1, shift C2 forward through the module’s book until it is unique from C1.
  • If C3 is the same as C1 or C2, shift C3 forward through the module’s book until it is unique from C1 and C2.
  • Discard one of the candidates, determined from the table on the next page using the last spell in the module’s book.
  • Perform the remaining two candidate spells and use the dagger while following the priority order below.

If there are NO even digits in the serial number:

  • N = number of batteries, kept in range 1-7 by adding/subtracting 7. Take the module’s book’s Nth spell and note its description.
  • Take the number of indicators, plus 1. Count this many words into the obtained description, starting again from the first word if you count past the last word.
    • If the obtained word is a numeral, take it as a word (eg. 1 → one). Hyphenated words are treated as two separate words. L = length of the found word.
  • Keep the number of ports in range 1-L by adding/subtracting L. Take this letter from the obtained word and the six letters that follow it. Treat the first letter of the description as coming after the last letter.

Perform the actions corresponding to the obtained letters in order, using the table below.

If an action has already been performed, perform the next action in the table (wrapping around if needed).
