On the Subject of Allmighty Sinnoh

“The rightful bearer of a Plate draws from the Plate it holds.”

All eighteen Mythical Plates appear before thee.

Thou shall determine which three Plates to summon by way of Judgement; before solving those three Plates’ tests.

To commence the Judgement, thou shall find the three Marked Plates:

  1. Marked by Time: The Plate visually lagging behind upon bomb movement.
  2. Marked by Space: The Plate not following the flipping motion of others.
  3. Marked by Antimatter: The Plate of which the name appears misspelled.

Thou will apply ten steps in order, moving each Marks using the order on Page 2.
Once all ten steps have been effected, Judgement is complete and thou shall press the three Marked Plates in order: Time, then Space, then Antimatter.
If at any point two Marks lie on the same Plate, offset once the most recent one.
