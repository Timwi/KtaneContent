On the Subject of Glass Cactus

Modern Art really is becoming advanced.

The Glass Cactus on the module is imprisoning a subtle breeze. Remove the correct spikes in order to release the breeze without disturbing it.

On the cactus, find the most and least present spike colours. In the diagram below, start at the "most"-coloured outer spike, go towards the center until the "least"-coloured spike, then go clockwise until the next "least"-coloured spike.

The breeze will show a sequence of 5 movements, resetting when it starts spinning. Move in the diagram below in the same directions, one spike at a time, where "down" is going towards the center, noting down the colours you land on (starting with the first movement).

Find this sequence of colours on the cactus, as a continuous line of spikes going in the same clock direction as the breeze's spins, able to switch rings but not to skip them. Remove those spikes in order to solve the module.
