On the Subject of Meadow Plate

One breath of this plate once triggered an allergic reaction that sent the poor soul directly to the Shadow Realm.

The plate on the module is inscribed with the name of four plants that you want to grow.
It also can be pressed in one of twelve spots, corresponding to the twelve months of the year.

Months Button Position
January February March
April May June
July August September
October November December

Find below the four plants in Appendix BERRIES, and submit a month where they all can be planted without issue to solve the module.

There is however one month that is Voided in your reality.
If the Voided month is one where a plant is marked as “plantable”, extend that plantation period by one month. Otherwise it is unaffected.
Leave other plantation periods unaffected.

For example, if a plant is plantable in February, March, April and September, and the Voided month is March; its Feb-Mar-Apr gets extended to May while its Sep doesn’t get affected by the Void.
Note that December is continuous with January.

To determine which month is Voided, sum all Serial Number digits, and subtract or add 12 until it is in the range 1-12. The corresponding month is the one that is Voided for you (January being 1 and December being 12).
