Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes Mod KTane Connections

On the Subject of KTane Connections

I could simply list which modules belong to each category, but that’s just asking for a big red prohibited icon.

This module has sixteen module icons in four different groups. Find what attributes the modules have in common, and select them in their groups. The possible categories and descriptions are below:

BossesFull boss modules
VanillaRegular modules from the original, vanilla game
NeediesNeedy modules (not including regular modules with the needy background)
Instant DeathModules that have the Instant death quirk
Red ModulesModules with red backgrounds or borders
Wire ModulesModules featuring multiple wires
SolosModules that can be done without the manual with little to no memorization
Card ModulesModules involving cards in some way (not limited to playing cards)
Morse ModulesModules that specifically use Morse code in some way
Based on GamesModules based on already existing board, card, or video games
Cipher ModulesModules involving an encrypted word or message OR characters being shown/represented in encrypted ways
Time-DependentModules that require input at a specific time
Navigation ModulesModules involving moving around a maze or diagram
Not ModulesModules with “Not” in their name
Grid ModulesModules primarily involving a visible grid
Single Button ModulesModules that have one main button or selectable
Non-Default BackgroundsModules that do not have the usual, gray-bluish metal background or border
