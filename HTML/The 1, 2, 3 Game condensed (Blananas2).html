On the Subject of The 1, 2, 3 Game

Congratulations, you have been invited to The genuis! Game.

  • Obtain two lists of numbers, one for the opponent's name and the other for the opponent's avatar.
  • Put these lists against eachother. The higher number at each index earns that list a point. Ties are ignored.
  • Use the list that earned more points. If there's a tie, the list that earned all its points earlier is used.
  • For each value in the used list in order, play the card whose number is one higher than the value.
    One higher than 3 wraps around to 1.
