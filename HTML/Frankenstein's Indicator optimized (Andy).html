On the Subject of Frankenstein’s Status Light

Where am I supposed to look??? “FRK’s indicator’s indicator.”

The module consists of a face with two eyes. Use the eyes for navigation and the nametag for the submission.

To determine the starting location, find the emoticon on the status light in the below table. To determine the target location, use the row given by the batteries, and column given by the indicators. If this cell is empty: move in the direction given by the number of ports mod 4. 0: Up, 1: Right, 2: Down, 3: Left.
