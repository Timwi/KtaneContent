On the Subject of Genre Divination

Don't worry, nobody understands what any of those words mean either.

The play button on this module will play a music sequence made of 4 tracks: Drums, Bass, Chords and Lead. Determine which variation of each track you are listening to.

After the module is solved, it will play tracks at random.

To solve this module, submit the Genre Name of the music you're listening to.
It consists of 3 parts: Prefix, Adjective, and Style.
Those parts are separated in five distinct "VST Banks", each containing five names for each parts; as described in Appendix BEATS.

The Bass, Chords and Lead tell you, for each parts, which VST Bank to look at.
The Drums tell you, within each VST Bank, which egdework element to look at to determine the correct answer.
If a value is 0, use 1. If a value is above 5, use 5.

The selected VST Banks on the module are visible by the number of colored bars above each text; as well as the text's background colour.
