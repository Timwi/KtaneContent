Colored Squares Identification reference

List out the number of times each distinct color appears, then sort the numbers in your list from lowest to highest.

Find the exact list you've obtained or an applicable condition regarding your list of numbers to identify the module you're looking for.

The conditions on that page take priority over conditions on this page.
