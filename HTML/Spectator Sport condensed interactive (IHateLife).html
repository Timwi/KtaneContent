On the Subject of Spectator Sport

“Oh dear, I’ve made quite a mess.”

When a non-ignored module is solved, 2-4 kill messages will be shown. Note these down.

Once every non-ignored module has been solved, questions about the match will be asked. Answer these questions to solve the module.

Note these special rules:

  • When A kills B four times without dying, they become B's nemesis. If B then manages to kill A, the kill counts twice and the nemesis effect is removed. This is handled automatically by the interactive.
  • The default game mode is Arena. However, if a notification references 'the intelligence' or 'the Control Point', the game mode is Capture the Intelligence or Capture Point, respectively. This doesn't change the "which team won" question, whichever team has the higher score wins, regardless of gamemode.

Question Reference

"Which player had the most kills in the round?"
The player with the highest kill (K) stat. Self-explanatory.

"Which player perished the most this round?"
The player with the highest death (D) stat.

"Which team has gained the victory in this round?"
The team with the higher kill count. Gamemode is irrelevant.

"Which gamemode was everyone playing?"
By default is "Arena", else is whatever was stated. Read, people.

"Which weapon was the most employed?"
The weapon that was used the most times to kill. Self-explanatory.

"How many critical hits were in this round?"
The total crit (C) stat from both teams.

"Which player managed to get the least amount of kills in the round?"
The player with the lowest kill stat from either team.

"How many players did [A] manage to deprive of life?"
The kill stat of player [A].

"How many times did [A] undergo the permanent cessation of all vital functions?"
The death stat of player [A].

"How many times was the [weapon] used to kill an enemy player?"

"How many times did player [A] kill player [B]?"
