On the Subject of Captcha The Flag

I think I prefer the “Select all images with scooters” captcha over this... Besides, are we sure Captcha is not a robot?

The module displays 6 characters from left to right. Consider the semaphore for each character in reading order.

If the character in your CAPTCHA is a part of ‘F1AG’, flip both flags for the character vertically. If the serial number character in the same position as the character you are currently translating is part of ‘PØLE’, flip both flags for the character horizontally.

If both of them applied: if the character in your CAPTCHA is F and the character in the same position in the serial number is P or L hold the blue flag, otherwise hold the red flag.

Once the image is faded press the green button to progress to the next stage.
