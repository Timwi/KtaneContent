On the Subject of Net Positive

When my wife lets me fish all day!

On each activation, the module provides buttons which represent a net of a cube, which when folded, will have two faces that overlap.

Pressing a button while the timer is active toggles whether that button is lit.

Only the two buttons that represent the overlapping faces should be lit up when the timer runs out to avoid a strike.
