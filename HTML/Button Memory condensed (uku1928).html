On the Subject of Button Memory

An ordinary match in all of the crazy matches.

Perform the correct action on the correct button to progress the module to the next stage. Complete all stages to disarm the module.
Pressing an incorrect button or releasing the correct button at the wrong time will result in a strike and reset the module back to stage 1.
Button positions are ordered from left to right.

Stage 1

  • ABORT ⇒ hold position 4
  • DETONATE ⇒ tap blue
  • HOLD ⇒ tap position 1
  • PRESS ⇒ hold position 2

Stage 2

  • ABORT ⇒ tap stage 1 color
  • DETONATE ⇒ hold stage 1 label
  • HOLD ⇒ tap stage 1 position
  • PRESS ⇒ hold blue

Stage 3

  • ABORT ⇒ tap stage 2 label
  • DETONATE ⇒ tap stage 1 position
  • HOLD ⇒ hold stage 2 color
  • PRESS ⇒ hold stage 1 label

Stage 4

  • ABORT ⇒ hold stage 2 color
  • DETONATE ⇒ hold stage 1 position
  • HOLD ⇒ tap stage 2 label
  • PRESS ⇒ tap stage 3 position

Stage 5

  • ABORT ⇒ tap stage 4 color
  • DETONATE ⇒ hold stage 3 color
  • HOLD ⇒ hold stage 1 label
  • PRESS ⇒ tap stage 2 label

Releasing A Held Button

  • Red ⇒ 6
  • Yellow ⇒ 3
  • Blue ⇒ 8
  • Other ⇒ 4
