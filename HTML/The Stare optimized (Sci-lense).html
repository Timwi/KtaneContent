Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes Mod The Stare

On the Subject of Staring at the Stares

Look into the eyes of your doom...

For those who are familiar with the original manual

Interact with every eye and toggle them to the correct state.

Desired States:

Red Normal or Special-Plain | Close
Small or Special-Rifted | Open
Even amount of Special-Warped | Open
Burgundy Eye/Background combo XNOR Other Eyes | Open
Gold Small XOR (Initial Bomb Time > Number of Eyes) | Close
Yellow & Purple More of either color | Open Most, Close Least
Else, Tie and in Indicators: Prospit > Derse | Open Yellows
Else, Tie and in Indicators: Derse > Prospit | Open Purples
Else | Open both colors
Green Non-needy module amount % (Plain:3 | Warped:5 | Rifted:7) = 0 | Open
Turquoise (Unique Eye XOR Unique Background) XOR Even letters of Turquoise in serial | Open
Gray X = (1 * Small) + (2 * Normal) + (3 * Special) + (5 * Rifted) + (7 * Warped) % 50
If X / 3 XOR X / 7 | Close
White Closed Eyes > Open Eyes | Open

Exception: Serial is ###DD#, close all eyes

Time Conditions

  1. Open
  2. Unique Color
  3. Two Even Digits in Serial
  4. ≥ 3 Eye Colors
  5. Normal NOR Plain
  6. Solved Modules / 5
  7. Green, Gray, or Gold
  8. Small XOR (Red NOR Burgundy)
  9. = 8 Eyes Total
  10. None Above Applies

Toggles: Toggle with odd amount of valid digits
