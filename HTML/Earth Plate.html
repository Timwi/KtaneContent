Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes Mod Earth Plate

On the Subject of Earth Plate

Infused with mud, salt, and the power of a creator deity.

The plate on this module allows you to move through Grid STAMINA using movements described below it.

You will start in the top-left coloured cell (blue), and your goal is to land onto the bottom-right coloured cell (red).
You need to land on the ending cell at the end of your movement, not during.

A Voided cell is considered to not exist. For more information about Voided Cells, see Shared Appendix V O I D.

You cannot wrap around the grid.

Look into Table CLYDESDALE, for each Port type, if it is present on the bomb, Void in Grid STAMINA the column and row marked with this number.

Table CLYDESDALE:

Port Name:SerialStereo RCAPS/2DVI-DRJ-45Parallel
Lines to void:1 2 3 4 5 6

Attempting to move outside of the bounds of the Grid will issue a Strike but NOT move you.
You are allowed to move outside the bounds of the Grid only if those cells are Voided and the cell you land on is in bounds.
As your movements consist of 3 individual steps (L-shaped movement), every individual step must land in bounds for the movement to be valid.

In case it is needed, press the “EARTH” text on the module’s casing to reset you to the starting position.

Shared Appendix V O I D:

Voided cells are considered to not exist anymore. Cells aligned on either sides are considered adjacent and at a distance of 1. This includes diagonals.

You can move from one side of a Voided cell to another in a single movement. You cannot stop a movement onto a Voided cell.

Neighbors of a Voided Cell are the cells directly adjacent to it (unless one of them is a Voided cell, in which case the neighbors are of course one cell further).
