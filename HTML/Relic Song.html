Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes Mod Relic Song

On the Subject of Relic Song

“When I feel a little sour, when I feel I got no power left...”

The module consists of a sheet music with a black clef-looking piece on its side.

This module has two Forms: one with the sheet music green and clef on the left (Aria), the other with the sheet music orange and clef on the right (Pirouette).

Every time a non-ignored module is solved, a series of 3-5 Notes will appear on the lines of the stave, with the current Mode potentially changing.

For every stage, record the Notes’ heights (the horizontal placement doesn’t matter) as well as the Mode they appear in, and apply the following rules for every Note:

  • If a Note has appeared in both Forms, ignore both appearances as they Cancel each other out.
  • If a Note appears multiple times in the same Mode without having been Canceled yet, ignore the duplicate appearances in this Mode.
  • For both Forms, keep track of all Note heights that have appeared but have not been Canceled out nor ignored.

When all other non-ignored modules have been solved, the clef will return to the left, the sheet music will show all five possible Note heights as empty circles, and the module will enter its Submission Mode.

Input all Note heights that have appeared in the current Mode but have not been Canceled out nor ignored.
After inputting the correct Notes, press the clef to switch Mode.
Once both Forms have been filled with the correct Notes, press the clef once more to submit and solve the module.

Pressing a wrong Note or switching Mode without all expected Notes being pressed will result in a Strike, and the module will enter its Recovery Mode.

While in Recovery, the module will show every stage that has appeared in order.
Interact with the Clef to cut the Recovery short and enter the Submission Mode.
