On the Subject of Insect Plate

Our battle will be Legendary!... Or not...

This module asks questions about Solves, Time and Strikes.

Consider the diagram found at Appendix SWARM. Start at the bottom node named A.

Every named node refers to a question found below, its answer tells you where in the diagram to move to.

When landing on a Node with a direction (Up, Down, Left, Right, Center), press the corresponding button on the Plate to submit it and solve the module.

Take all Letters in the Serial Number, and Void the associated Node (except A).
Voided Nodes are considered to not exist. If you land on a Voided Node, keep moving using the same answer (true / false) you got at the previous Node, regardless of the actual answer.

List of Questions:

Please note that the validity of the question is verified upon submission.

  • A) Is the last digit of the Serial Number even?
  • B) Are there 10 or more non-needy modules?
  • C) Are there 3 or more batteries?
  • D) Is the module Simon’s Spider present?
  • E) Is there a BOB Indicator?
  • F) Is there a PS/2 port?
  • G) Is over 50% of the time remaining?
  • H) Is the module Flyswatting present?
  • I) Are there 0 solved modules?
  • J) Does the bomb have 1 or more strikes?
  • K) Is the letter E present in the Serial Number?
  • L) Is the module Langton’s Ant present?
  • M) Is this plate summoned by the module Allmighty Sinnoh?
  • N) Is there an empty Port Plate?
