Section A: Introduction

The Crazy Talk module consists of a digital display that shows text and a two-position switch.

Objective: Read the text on the display, look up the required seconds timing from the reference table, and flip the switch down and back up when the bomb timer matches the left and right sides of the slash.

Section B: Step by Step

  1. Text will appear on the display.
  2. Find the exact match of the text and its action timer in the reference table.
  3. Flip the switch down when the bomb timer’s seconds digits match the number on the left side of the slash.
  4. Flip the switch back up when the bomb timer’s seconds digits match the number on the right side of the slash.

Section D: Experting Tips

Some phrases are closely similar to each other, and you should not underestimate them. Pay careful attention to specific words like “RISKY” versus “WHISKEY” in phonetic spelling strings.

Example:

  • RISKY: LIMA ECHO FOXTROT TANGO SPACE ALPHA ROMEO ROMEO OSCAR RISKY SPACE SIERRA YANKEE MIKE BRAVO OSCAR LIMA (Timer: 1/2)
  • WHISKEY: LIMA ECHO FOXTROT TANGO SPACE ALPHA ROMEO ROMEO OSCAR WHISKEY SPACE SIERRA YANKEE MIKE BRAVO OSCAR LIMA (Timer: 6/5)

Section E: Disarming and Strikes

  • Strike: Flipping the switch down or up at the wrong seconds value, or interacting incorrectly.
  • Disarm: Successfully flipping the switch down and up at the exact target seconds specified by the table for the given phrase.

Section F: Example Solve

  • The display shows: STOP STOP.
  • Looking up STOP STOP in the table reveals the timer code 7/5.
  • Wait until the bomb timer seconds digit matches the number on the left side of the slash (7), then flip the switch down.
  • Wait until the bomb timer seconds digit matches the number on the right side of the slash (5), then flip the switch back up to disarm the module.
