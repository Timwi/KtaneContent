Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes Mod Hectic Arithmetic

On the Subject of Hectic Arithmetic

The calculations are ephemeral...

  • The module initially shows a clock with seven blank displays, along with four buttons with standard mathematical operators that cannot be pressed.
  • Once the clock is pressed, the module will reveal six numbers, along with a target number.
  • You will have 30 seconds to make the target number from the smaller numbers. There will always be at least one solution. Although it is not required, it is also always possible to reach the target without ever making a number larger than 99.
  • Pressing an operator will apply it to the first two smaller numbers.
  • The operators are always applied to the smaller numbers in order, and the first two available smaller numbers are replaced with the result when performing a valid calculation.
  • Once a calculation is done, it cannot be undone.
  • If you attempt to create a number that is not an integer, is negative, or is larger than 999, the calculation will not be accepted and the clock will keep ticking. This will not incur a strike.
  • If the countdown clock expires, a strike will be incurred, and the module will return to its initial state. New numbers will be generated the next time the clock is pressed.
