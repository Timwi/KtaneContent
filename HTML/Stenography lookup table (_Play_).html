Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes Mod Stenography

On the Subject of Stenography

Typing at an astonishing three words per bomb!

For those familiar with the original manual.

  • To start the module, press the * key.
  • Then, within 30 seconds, three times:
    • Read the word on the module’s display (which is written in a 3-pixel-tall font).
    • Input the stroke of the displayed word (obtained from the table on the next page).
    • Press the “RELEASE” button.
