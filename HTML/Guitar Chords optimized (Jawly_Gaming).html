On the Subject of Guitar Chords

You hum it, I’ll play it.

  • The module consists of a guitar fretboard, three level displays and a play button.
  • To defuse the module, you must enter the finger positions of the given chords at the capo position.
  • Entering a wrong or invalid chord will cause a strike.
  • Press a fret to select it. Press it again to deselect it.
  • At each level, the capo positions are determined by the tables below. Use the first condition that applies.

Instruction

  • Add the determined capo position to the chord positions of the displayed chord. Some chord positions are "_" and should be ignored
  • Start at the rightmost position on the fretboard (index 1).
  • For each string from top to bottom, take the corresponding digit from the chord position. If it is a number, press the fret that many frets from the right (1 = the rightmost fret). Otherwise leave them empty.
  • Continue with other chord positions for each chord position, moving from the top string to the bottom.
  • Repeat the process for levels 2 and 3 to solve this.
